# Standard Operating Procedure: Motor Insurance Claims

**Document**: SOP-MOTOR-001  
**Effective Date**: 2024-01-01  
**Owner**: Claims Operations  
**SYNTHETIC — FOR DEVELOPMENT ONLY**

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the step-by-step process for handling motor insurance claims from intake to closure.

---

## 2. Claim Intake (Target: within 24 hours of intimation)

1. Verify policy is active and premium is paid up to date
2. Confirm the claimant is the registered policy holder or authorised driver
3. Collect preliminary information:
   - Date, time, and location of incident
   - Nature of damage (own damage / third party / theft)
   - Whether FIR has been filed (mandatory for theft and third-party)
4. Issue Claim Reference Number (CRN)
5. Send acknowledgement to claimant via SMS/email

---

## 3. Document Collection (Target: within 48 hours)

**Mandatory for all claims:**
- Claim form (signed)
- Policy document copy
- Vehicle RC copy
- Driver's licence (valid at time of incident)

**Additional for own damage:**
- Repair estimate from authorised workshop
- Photographs of damage (all angles)

**Additional for theft:**
- FIR (original)
- All vehicle keys
- RTO intimation letter
- Non-traceable certificate (after 90 days)

**Additional for third-party:**
- FIR (original)
- Legal notice received (if any)
- Court documents (if proceedings initiated)

---

## 4. Survey Appointment

1. **Claims < Rs 20,000**: Cashless direct settlement at network garage. No survey needed.
2. **Claims Rs 20,000 – 5,00,000**: Appoint empanelled surveyor within 48 hours.
3. **Claims > Rs 5,00,000**: Appoint independent IRDAI-licensed surveyor within 24 hours.
4. **Total loss**: Specialist surveyor; joint inspection with insured required.

---

## 5. Coverage Verification Checklist

- [ ] Incident falls within policy period
- [ ] Cause of loss is a covered peril (Section 2.1)
- [ ] No applicable exclusions (Section 4.1)
- [ ] Driver had valid licence at time of incident
- [ ] Driver was not under influence of alcohol/drugs
- [ ] Vehicle was not used for hire or reward (unless commercial policy)

---

## 6. Fraud Screening

Mandatory fraud check for all claims. Escalate to FRAUD_REVIEW if any of the following:
- Claim intimated < 30 days after policy inception
- Claimed amount > 1.5× IDV
- Driver's licence mismatch
- Same vehicle claimed more than twice in 12 months
- Incident location inconsistent with policy address zone

---

## 7. Settlement

| Scenario | Settlement Basis | Timeline |
|----------|-----------------|---------|
| Cashless at network garage | Direct payment to garage | Within 7 days of survey |
| Reimbursement | Bank transfer to insured | Within 15 working days |
| Total loss | IDV less deductible | Within 30 days of all docs |
| Theft | IDV (after non-traceable cert) | Within 30 days of certificate |

---

## 8. Escalation Matrix

| Trigger | Escalate To | TAT |
|---------|------------|-----|
| Settlement > Rs 5,00,000 | Senior Adjudicator | Immediate |
| Fraud score > 0.7 | Fraud Review Team | Same day |
| Disputed liability | Legal team | Within 24 hours |
| Claimant complaint | Grievance Cell | Within 48 hours |
