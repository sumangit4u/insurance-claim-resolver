# Health Insurance Policy — Standard Terms and Conditions

**Policy Type**: Individual and Family Floater Health Insurance  
**Insurer**: Bharati General Insurance Co. Ltd. (SYNTHETIC — FOR DEVELOPMENT ONLY)  
**Version**: 2024.1

---

## Section 1: Definitions

### 1.1 Sum Insured
The maximum amount payable per policy year per insured person (or family unit for floater).

### 1.2 Pre-existing Disease (PED)
Any condition, ailment, or injury for which the insured had signs/symptoms or was diagnosed before policy commencement.

### 1.3 Waiting Period
The period from policy commencement during which specified benefits are not payable.

### 1.4 Network Hospital
A hospital that has an agreement with the insurer for cashless treatment.

---

## Section 2: In-patient Hospitalisation

### 2.1 Coverage
All medically necessary hospitalisation for more than **24 hours** is covered, including:
- Room rent (up to 1% of Sum Insured per day; ICU up to 2%)
- Surgeon, anaesthetist, and specialist fees
- Medicines, consumables, and implants
- Pre-hospitalisation expenses up to **30 days** before admission
- Post-hospitalisation expenses up to **60 days** after discharge

### 2.2 Day Care Procedures
138 specified day care procedures covered without the 24-hour requirement (see Annexure A).

---

## Section 3: Exclusions

### 3.1 Waiting Periods
- **30-day waiting period**: All illnesses except accidental injuries
- **2-year waiting period**: Specific diseases (hernia, cataract, joint replacement, etc.)
- **4-year waiting period**: Pre-existing diseases

### 3.2 Permanent Exclusions
- Cosmetic or aesthetic treatments
- Self-inflicted injuries or suicide attempts
- Experimental or unproven treatments
- Dental treatment (unless due to accident)
- Spectacles, hearing aids, external prostheses

---

## Section 4: Out-patient Department (OPD)

### 4.1 OPD Coverage (Rider)
Available as an optional rider with coverage up to Rs 10,000 per year for:
- Doctor consultations
- Diagnostic tests and investigations
- Pharmacy (prescription medications only)

### 4.2 Chronic Disease Management
For Sum Insured ≥ Rs 5,00,000: OPD for management of listed chronic conditions covered up to Rs 15,000/year.
Covered conditions include: Diabetes (Type 1 & 2), Hypertension, Asthma, COPD.

---

## Section 5: Critical Illness (Optional Rider)

### 5.1 Covered Conditions
Lump-sum benefit of Rs 5,00,000–50,00,000 on first diagnosis of:
- Cancer of specified severity
- Heart attack (first occurrence)
- Stroke resulting in permanent neurological deficit
- Kidney failure requiring dialysis
- Major organ transplant

### 5.2 Survival Period
The insured must survive **30 days** after diagnosis to receive the benefit.

---

## Section 6: Claims Procedure

### 6.1 Cashless Claims
1. Identify network hospital (check insurer website/app)
2. Present health card at hospital help desk
3. Hospital submits pre-authorisation to insurer (within 4 hours for planned; immediately for emergency)
4. Insurer approves/rejects within **2 hours** (emergency) or **4 hours** (planned)

### 6.2 Reimbursement Claims
1. Settle bills at hospital
2. Submit claim within **30 days** of discharge
3. Required documents: discharge summary, all bills, investigation reports, pharmacy receipts

### 6.3 Settlement Timeline
Reimbursement claims settled within **15 working days** of receiving complete documents.

---

## Section 7: No Claim Bonus

- 10% increase in Sum Insured for each claim-free year
- Maximum cumulative increase: 50% of base Sum Insured
- Bonus reversed proportionally for each claim year

---

## Section 8: Portability

Policyholders may port to another insurer at renewal without losing waiting period credits, subject to IRDAI portability guidelines.
