# Motor Insurance Policy — Standard Terms and Conditions

**Policy Type**: Comprehensive Motor Insurance  
**Insurer**: Bharati General Insurance Co. Ltd. (SYNTHETIC — FOR DEVELOPMENT ONLY)  
**Version**: 2024.1

---

## Section 1: Definitions

### 1.1 Insured Vehicle
The vehicle described in the Schedule and for which a premium has been paid.

### 1.2 Insured Event
Any sudden, unforeseen, and accidental damage to or loss of the Insured Vehicle.

### 1.3 Third Party
Any person other than the policyholder, their immediate family members, or employees.

### 1.4 Total Loss
Damage exceeding 75% of the Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle.

---

## Section 2: Scope of Cover

### 2.1 Own Damage Cover
The policy covers accidental damage to the Insured Vehicle caused by:
- Collision or overturning
- Fire, explosion, or lightning
- Burglary, housebreaking, or theft
- Flood, inundation, cyclone, or other natural calamities
- Riot, strike, or malicious act

### 2.2 Third Party Liability
The policy covers legal liability arising from:
- Bodily injury or death of a third party caused by the Insured Vehicle
- Property damage to a third party, up to Rs 7,50,000

### 2.3 Personal Accident Cover
- Owner-driver: Rs 15,00,000 on death or permanent disability
- Unnamed passengers: Rs 2,00,000 per passenger (optional rider)

---

## Section 3: Claims Procedure

### 3.1 Intimation
The insured must intimate the claim within **7 days** of the incident. Late intimation may result in claim rejection.

### 3.2 Required Documents
- Duly filled claim form
- Original policy document
- Registration Certificate (RC) of the vehicle
- Driving Licence of the driver at time of incident
- FIR (mandatory for theft and third-party claims)
- Repair estimate from an authorised workshop
- Photographs of damage

### 3.3 Survey
An independent surveyor will be appointed within **48 hours** of intimation for damage assessment.

### 3.4 Cashless Claims
Available at network garages. Insurer pays the garage directly after deductible.

### 3.5 Reimbursement Claims
Payable within **15 working days** of submission of all documents.

---

## Section 4: Exclusions

### 4.1 Standard Exclusions
The policy does NOT cover:
- Normal wear and tear or mechanical/electrical breakdown
- Damage when driving under influence of alcohol or drugs
- Damage outside the geographical area of India
- Consequential losses (loss of business, depreciation in value)
- Damage to tyres/tubes (except where vehicle is also damaged)

### 4.2 Depreciation Schedule
| Component | Depreciation Rate |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Plastic/rubber parts | 50% |
| Fibre glass components | 30% |
| Metal parts (vehicle < 5 years) | 0–35% (sliding scale) |
| Metal parts (vehicle > 5 years) | 40–50% |

### 4.3 Compulsory Deductible
- Vehicles < 1500 cc: Rs 1,000
- Vehicles ≥ 1500 cc: Rs 2,000
- Commercial vehicles: Rs 3,000

---

## Section 5: Theft Claims

### 5.1 Procedure
1. File FIR with police immediately
2. Intimate insurer within 24 hours
3. Submit all vehicle keys to insurer
4. Obtain non-traceable certificate from police after 90 days
5. Transfer RC to insurer upon settlement

### 5.2 Settlement Basis
Theft settlements are paid at the **Insured Declared Value (IDV)** minus the compulsory deductible.

---

## Section 6: No Claim Bonus (NCB)

| Claim-Free Years | NCB on Own Damage Premium |
|-----------------|--------------------------|
| 1 year | 20% |
| 2 years | 25% |
| 3 years | 35% |
| 4 years | 45% |
| 5+ years | 50% |

NCB is forfeited upon making a claim in that policy year.

---

## Section 7: Dispute Resolution

### 7.1 Internal Escalation
Disputes must first be raised with the insurer's Grievance Cell within 30 days of claim decision.

### 7.2 Insurance Ombudsman
If unresolved within 30 days, the insured may approach the Insurance Ombudsman (IRDAI).

### 7.3 Arbitration
For disputes exceeding Rs 20 lakhs, arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
