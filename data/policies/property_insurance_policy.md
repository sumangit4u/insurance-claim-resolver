# Property Insurance Policy — Standard Terms and Conditions

**Policy Type**: Standard Fire and Special Perils / Property All Risk  
**Insurer**: Bharati General Insurance Co. Ltd. (SYNTHETIC — FOR DEVELOPMENT ONLY)  
**Version**: 2024.1

---

## Section 1: Definitions

### 1.1 Insured Property
The building, contents, and/or stock described in the Schedule.

### 1.2 Sum Insured
The reinstatement value of the property at the time of the incident.

### 1.3 Reinstatement Value
Cost of rebuilding or replacing the property to the same condition as when new.

### 1.4 Market Value
Reinstatement value less depreciation for age and usage.

---

## Section 2: Scope of Cover

### 2.1 Standard Fire and Special Perils
Covers loss or damage caused by:
- Fire, lightning, explosion/implosion
- Aircraft damage
- Riot, strike, malicious damage
- Storm, cyclone, typhoon, tempest, hurricane, tornado, flood, and inundation
- Impact damage (vehicles, animals)
- Subsidence and landslide
- Bursting/overflowing of water tanks, pipes
- Missile testing operations
- Bush fire

### 2.2 Burglary and Housebreaking (Rider)
Covers loss of contents due to forcible entry. Not applicable to theft without force.

### 2.3 Business Interruption (Rider)
Covers loss of gross profit due to interruption of business following an insured peril.
Indemnity period: up to 12 months.

---

## Section 3: Claims Procedure

### 3.1 Intimation
Must be made within **7 days** of the incident.

### 3.2 Documentation Required
- Claim form duly completed
- FIR (for fire, burglary, riot)
- Photographs of damage (before any repair work)
- Repair estimates from at least two contractors
- List of damaged/destroyed items with purchase invoices
- Fire brigade report (for fire claims)
- Meteorological report (for storm/flood claims)

### 3.3 Survey
- Claims < Rs 1,00,000: desktop settlement (documents only)
- Claims Rs 1,00,000–10,00,000: independent surveyor appointed within 48 hours
- Claims > Rs 10,00,000: specialist loss assessor appointed; joint inspection mandatory

### 3.4 Settlement Basis
- Buildings: Reinstatement basis (like for like)
- Contents: Market value (reinstatement less depreciation)
- Stock: Market value at date of loss

---

## Section 4: Exclusions

### 4.1 Standard Exclusions
The policy does NOT cover:
- Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, mechanical or electrical breakdown
- War, invasion, nuclear perils
- Willful act or negligence of the insured
- Property in course of construction
- Loss discovered only at stocktaking (not traceable to a specific event)
- Unexplained loss or shortage

### 4.2 Underinsurance
If the Sum Insured is less than the actual value of the property, the insured is considered a co-insurer and will bear a proportional share of any loss.

**Formula**: Claim Payable = (Sum Insured / Actual Value) × Loss Amount

### 4.3 Depreciation Rates (Contents)
| Age of Item | Depreciation |
|-------------|-------------|
| 0–1 year | 10% |
| 1–3 years | 20–30% |
| 3–5 years | 35–50% |
| > 5 years | 50–70% |

---

## Section 5: High-Value Claims (> Rs 5,00,000)

### 5.1 Mandatory Requirements
- Joint survey with insurer representative
- Proof of ownership (purchase invoices, valuation certificate)
- Chartered Accountant certificate for stock losses

### 5.2 Advance Payment
For accepted claims > Rs 5,00,000, an advance of 50% may be released within 10 working days pending final assessment.

---

## Section 6: Reinstatement Conditions

### 6.1 Reinstatement Requirement
For reinstatement basis claims, repairs must begin within **12 months** of the loss and be completed within **24 months**.

### 6.2 Failure to Reinstate
If reinstatement does not begin within 12 months, the claim reverts to market value basis.
